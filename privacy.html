YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

This notice describes information handling visible in the current VC Fashion website and its integrations.

This website is operated under the VC Fashion brand by Vaishnavi collection.

Information used by the store

  • When you create or use an account, account and sign-in information is handled through Supabase Authentication. The Supabase client is configured to persist the sign-in session in your browser.
  • For an order, checkout collects your name, email, phone number and delivery address, city, state and postal code. Product, variant, quantity, order total, payment state and order references are also used to create and display the order.
  • The cart and wishlist are stored in browser local storage. Checkout may temporarily store entered delivery details in session storage so they can be restored if you sign in during checkout.
  • The catalogue page requests public product, category and variant data from Supabase. Product catalogue data is used to display the store.

Payment processing

VC Fashion loads Razorpay Checkout to initiate online payment. The checkout sends order and customer prefill information to Razorpay. The website then sends the payment response to its Supabase Edge Functions for server-side verification. Payment-instrument details are entered in the Razorpay payment experience; do not send card, bank or UPI credentials to VC Fashion support.

Service providers and external resources

The website uses Supabase for authentication, catalogue and order services, Razorpay for payment checkout, and Cloudflare Pages for website hosting. Google Fonts and jsDelivr are requested to load fonts and the Supabase browser library. These providers receive technical request information as part of serving their services. Their own privacy and retention practices are governed by their respective policies.

Links to social networks or WhatsApp open those services only when you choose to follow a link.

Browser storage, cookies and analytics

The website uses local storage and session storage as described above. A review of the project code found no explicit analytics integration or first-party cookie-based tracking. Cookie or retention behavior of hosting, authentication, payment and asset providers has not been independently verified.

Retention and questions

VC Fashion retains records for as long as required by applicable legal, tax and accounting requirements. Other personal information should be deleted when it is no longer needed for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable obligations. This policy does not claim a fixed retention schedule or automated deletion.
