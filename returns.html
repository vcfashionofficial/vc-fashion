WHEN SOMETHING ISN’T RIGHT

Return & Refund.

Eligibility is limited to damaged or incorrect items.

Eligible items

Only items that arrive damaged or incorrect are eligible for return or refund consideration.

How to request help

Contact VC Fashion within 3 days of delivery to report a damaged or incorrect item. You can use the email or phone number on our Contact Us page.

Evidence and review

Evidence is considered case by case. We may review photos or other evidence first and request the product back if needed. An unboxing video is not universally required.

Return courier

VC Fashion pays the return courier cost for an eligible damaged or incorrect item when a return is requested.

Refunds

Each case is reviewed manually, and the refund method is decided for that approved case. Our processing target is 3–5 business days after approval. This is a processing target, not a guaranteed date for funds to appear: bank and payment-provider credit times may differ.

For support, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.
