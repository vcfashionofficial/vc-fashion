Terms & Conditions.

These terms describe the main steps for using the VC Fashion online store.

Products and availability

Product descriptions, colours, sizes, prices and stock are shown in the current catalogue. Colours may appear differently on different screens. Availability can change, and an item is not reserved until an order is placed.

Prices and orders

Prices are displayed in Indian rupees. The product subtotal, shipping amount and final order total are shown at checkout before payment. Current shipping charges are described in our Shipping & Delivery Policy. We do not add a separate tax line in the current checkout.

Submitting checkout creates an order that remains pending until its payment is successfully verified by the server. Razorpay provides the payment checkout; VC Fashion does not treat a payment attempt alone as confirmation of a paid order.

Delivery

Estimated delivery is 3–7 business days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. See the Shipping & Delivery Policy for the shipping charge currently shown by checkout.

Returns and refunds

Only items that arrive damaged or incorrect are eligible for return or refund consideration. Report an eligible issue within 3 days of delivery. Evidence is reviewed case by case; photos or other evidence may be reviewed first, and the product may be requested back if needed. VC Fashion pays return courier costs for eligible damaged or incorrect items. Approved refunds have a processing target of 3–5 business days after approval. Bank or payment-provider credit times may differ, so an exact credit date is not guaranteed. The refund method is decided manually for each approved case. See our Return & Refund Policy for details.

Cancellation before dispatch

You may cancel an order before it is dispatched. Contact VC Fashion support promptly to request cancellation and confirm the dispatch status. Dispatch and tracking updates are provided manually according to the order. If payment has been made, any refund is reviewed and arranged manually; no automatic cancellation or refund timing is promised.

Support

For questions about an order or these terms, contact VC Fashion support. These terms do not change any rights that apply under law.
